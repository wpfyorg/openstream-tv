The ring is the o of openstream: one open ring sized to the width of the word’s own letters, sitting on the x-height band with its opening turned away from the word with the on-air dot held in it. Every specimen sits on a solid ground rather than a transparent pane, so nothing can disappear. Click any logo to open the original file.
